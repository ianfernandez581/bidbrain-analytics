Privacy notice
How filing works
  1. Invite the bot to a Slack channel (/invite @Bidbrain Knowledge). That invite is the only thing that puts a channel in scope - nothing else is read.
  2. Say whose channel it is in the table below. A mapped channel files every thread and every day's messages under that client's Slack folder on its own.
  3. An unmapped channel goes through the same steps as a meeting: memory, evidence, one AI read. Sure enough files itself; the rest waits here for a person.

Edits and deletions in Slack are honoured on the next sync - the library never holds a message Slack no longer holds. Remove the app from Slack and everything it filed is deleted. Everything here is internal.

What we tell people this app does, and what it stores, is on the public privacy notice. Send that link to anyone who asks.
