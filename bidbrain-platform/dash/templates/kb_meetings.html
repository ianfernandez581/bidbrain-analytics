How filing works
  1. A recording arrives from Fathom and the system reads who was on the call and what was said.
  2. If the client is certain - a known email domain or a person seen before - it is filed under that client's Meetings folder on its own.
  3. Otherwise it waits here with the system's best guess. A person confirms, and the system remembers the people and the title so the next one files itself.
