Meetings

How filing works

Recorded meetings arrive from Fathom and are filed into a client's Meetings folder as documents - summary first, then the transcript. A meeting whose client is certain (an invitee's email domain, or a person the system has already seen on that client's calls) is filed on its own. Anything else waits below for a person to confirm, with the system's best guess and its confidence shown. Confirming teaches the system, so the next meeting with the same people or the same recurring title files itself. Transcripts are internal; nothing on this page reaches a client.
