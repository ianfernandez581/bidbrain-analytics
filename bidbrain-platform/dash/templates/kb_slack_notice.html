Bidbrain Knowledge for Slack

What this app reads, what it stores, and for how long. Last updated 18 September 2026.

Bidbrain Knowledge is an internal tool built and used by 100% Digital. When someone invites it into a Slack channel, it files that channel's messages into our own knowledge base so our staff can search and ask questions about work we have already discussed.

What it can and cannot do

It reads only the channels it has been invited to. That invitation is the entire scope: a channel nobody has invited it into is invisible to it, and removing it from a channel ends its access to that channel.

It cannot read direct messages or group DMs, and it holds no permission to post, reply, react or message anyone. It never writes anything in Slack.

It records the names of files shared in a channel. It does not download them.

What is stored, and where

WhatMessage text, the author's display name, the channel name and the time the message was sent. Plus staff names and email addresses from the workspace directory, used to turn Slack user IDs into readable names.
WhereGoogle Cloud Storage, in Google's australia-southeast1 region (Sydney), in a private bucket owned by 100% Digital.
Who can read it100% Digital staff with knowledge-base access. It is never shown to a client, and never to another agency.
How longFor as long as the message exists in Slack. Editing or deleting a message in Slack updates or removes our copy on the next sync. Removing the app from the workspace deletes everything it holds within 14 business days.

Artificial intelligence

Passages from these messages are sent to AI providers so the assistant can answer a question and file a conversation under the right client. We use providers whose terms state that they do not train their models on what we send:

  • Google Gemini, paid API tier, which excludes prompts and responses from model improvement.
  • Anthropic Claude, commercial terms, which bar training on customer content and delete it within 30 days.
  • Google Vertex AI, for search indexing, which does not train on inputs.

Slack-derived text is deliberately withheld from any provider whose terms permit training on what it receives, whichever model a member of staff has selected. Today that means it is never sent to Moonshot's Kimi.

We do not sell this data, and we do not share it with anyone outside the providers above.

If you would rather a channel stayed out

Say so and we will not invite the app to it, or we will remove it. No configuration is needed anywhere: leaving the channel is what ends the access.
